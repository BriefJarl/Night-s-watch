## 🎯 The Problem

Border security forces already deploy CCTV cameras across **Border Out Posts (BOPs), check posts, border roads, and strategic locations**.

However, conventional CCTV systems mainly provide **video recording and live monitoring**, requiring continuous human observation.

Advanced capabilities such as **person tracking, ANPR, intrusion detection, facial analysis, and intelligent activity monitoring** often require expensive specialized hardware.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

**NIGHT'S WATCH** is an AI-driven software platform designed to run on top of existing IP-based CCTV infrastructure.

The platform transforms standard CCTV video streams into actionable intelligence using:

- Artificial Intelligence
- Machine Learning
- Computer Vision
- Real-Time Video Analytics

### 🎯 No Smart Cameras. No Dedicated Surveillance Hardware. Just Intelligent Software.
