# Sample Video Dataset

This folder contains sample CCTV-style video clips used for testing and demonstrating the NIGHT'S WATCH / IBVAP intelligent video analytics platform.

## Dataset Contents

The sample videos include different surveillance scenarios:

- Road and traffic surveillance
- City street views
- Vehicle movement
- Pedestrian movement
- Office/building areas
- Empty/deserted areas
- Night-time surveillance
- Parking and open areas
